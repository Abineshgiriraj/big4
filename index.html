Who we are

A specialist desk for accounting careers — from first articleship to the CFO's chair.

Based at the RUSA Incubation Centre, Bharathiar University, Coimbatore, we work as a 360-degree HR partner to accounting firms and finance teams. Every candidate is screened for technical depth, software fluency and professional etiquette before a profile ever reaches your desk.

For candidates, we open doors that job portals can't — direct mandates from partner firms hiring quietly, in India and across six other countries.
