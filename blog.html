What CA firms really look for in a fresher's resume

After screening fresher profiles for 20+ years, the pattern is unmistakable — the resumes that get callbacks share five quiet traits that have nothing to do with marks.

6 min readOct 2026

Every recruitment season, the same stack of resumes lands on a partner's desk — and within seconds, most are set aside. Not because the candidates lack marks, but because the resume fails to answer the only question a firm has: can this person start contributing?

The resumes that survive share five traits. First, they name the software — Tally, Excel, Zoho Books, whatever they genuinely know — instead of writing "computer skills". Second, they show any real exposure: a college GST workshop, a two-week internship, even maintaining accounts for a family business.

Third, they keep it to one page with clean formatting; a cluttered resume signals a cluttered ledger. Fourth, they state their articleship or job intent clearly — audit, taxation, or accounts — so the firm knows where to place them. And fifth, they proofread. A typo in an accountant's resume is a self-elimination.

Do these five things and you are already ahead of most of the stack. When you're ready, our desk can place your profile directly with firms that are hiring — quietly, and right now.

CA vs CMA vs CS (Inter): which articleship path fits you?

8 min readOct 2026

Three professional courses, three very different day-to-day realities. CA articleship immerses you in audit and taxation across dozens of clients — breadth first, depth later. CMA training leans toward cost accounting and manufacturing finance, often inside industry rather than practice. CS training centres on company law, compliance and board processes.

The right choice depends less on prestige and more on where you want to sit in ten years. Love client variety and advisory? CA firms. Drawn to factories, costing and ERP? CMA in industry. Fascinated by governance and regulation? CS.

Whichever path you choose, the firm you train with matters more than the letterhead's fame. A mid-sized firm where you touch real work beats a famous name where you photocopy for three years. Our trainee desk places CA / CMA / CS (Inter) students into firms we have personally vetted for training quality.

Working for US & UK CPA firms from India: a starter guide

7 min readSep 2026

Offshore accounting for US and UK CPA firms is one of the fastest-growing career lanes for Indian accountants — and it pays accordingly. But the skill bar is specific: US GAAP or UK FRS basics, tools like QuickBooks, Xero or Drake, and written English crisp enough for client-facing emails.

Time zones are the trade-off. Most US-facing roles run evening-to-late-night IST; UK and Australian shifts are gentler. Firms pay a premium for night-shift reliability, so decide honestly which rhythm suits your life.

Entry routes include junior offshore accountant roles, seasonal US tax preparation (January to April), and dedicated remote desks. Our global desk places candidates with CPA firms in the US, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Canada and South Africa — with proper contracts, not vague promises.

Why your best accounting hire is not on a job portal

5 min readSep 2026

The strongest senior accountants and auditors are rarely "actively looking". They are busy closing quarters for someone else — and they move only when a trusted voice puts the right opportunity in front of them.

That is the quiet math behind specialist recruitment. A portal gives you volume; a network gives you access. After 20+ years, our desk knows who is ready to move, what it would take, and whether their notice period fits your busy season.

If your last three portal hires didn't survive probation, the problem isn't the market — it's the channel. One mandate conversation is enough to see the difference.

From accounts trainee to finance manager: a realistic 8-year map

9 min readAug 2026

Years one to two: master the basics until they're boring — vouchers, reconciliations, Excel. Years three to four: own a client set or a process end-to-end; this is where GST, TDS and finalisation experience compounds.

Years five to six: supervise. Review juniors' work, talk to clients directly, and learn to say "I'll check and revert" with confidence. Years seven to eight: run the desk — targets, timelines and team.

The map rarely fails; skipping steps does. The fastest finance managers we have placed all did the slow years properly. Wherever you are on the map, there is a role in our current openings that fits the next step.

The 7-country map: where Indian accounting talent is hired most

6 min readAug 2026

India remains the deepest market — CA firms in every tier of city are hiring freshers and trainees. The US follows, with CPA firms building permanent offshore desks for bookkeeping, payroll and tax-season surges.

The UK and Australia hire for client accounting and BAS/returns work, often on friendlier time zones. New Zealand, Canada and South Africa complete the map — smaller volumes, but loyal firm relationships and excellent retention.

For candidates, the lesson is simple: global demand for Indian accounting talent is structural, not a trend. For firms abroad, one specialist desk in Coimbatore can staff an entire offshore team. Both sides are one call away.
