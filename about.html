Our story

A desk built on trust, not transactions

Big 4 Accounting Recruitment began with a simple observation: accounting firms don't struggle to find applicants — they struggle to find the right ones. Generic job portals flood inboxes; we chose the opposite path.

For over two decades we have specialised in one thing only — accounting talent. Freshers from B.Com, M.Com and MBA (Finance) programs. Accounts trainees. CA, CMA and CS (Inter) candidates seeking the right articleship. And at the other end of the ladder, the senior accountants, auditors, tax professionals, finance managers and CFOs that firms trust their clients with.

From our base at the RUSA Incubation Centre in Bharathiar University, we now serve CA & CPA firms in India, the US, the UK, Australia, New Zealand, Canada and South Africa.

“Every placement is a promise — to the firm that trusts us with its team, and to the candidate who trusts us with a career.”
What guides us

Three values, zero shortcuts

The same principles have carried every mandate for 20+ years.

Precision over volume

We send three right profiles, never thirty approximations. Your interview time is respected like billable hours.

Honesty, both ways

Realistic salary bands for firms, truthful role briefs for candidates. Trust compounds faster than any commission.

Global reach, local roots

Seven countries on the map, one office in Coimbatore. Every relationship still gets a personal phone call.

Like a pyramid, a firm is only as strong as the people at every level.

From the freshest article assistant to the signing partner — we staff every tier.

The person behind the desk

Rajkumar S M

HR Consultant — 20+ years into recruitment

Rajkumar has spent more than two decades inside accounting recruitment — long enough to watch article assistants he placed become partners at the firms that hired them.

His method is deliberately old-school: understand the firm's practice areas, meet the candidate, check the technical basics personally, and only then make an introduction. It's slower than an algorithm. It also works far better.

Today he advises CA & CPA firms across seven countries on building finance teams — from a single accounts trainee to a complete offshore accounting desk.
